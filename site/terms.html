Aurum Cloud

Aurum Cloud is a managed version of the AGPL-3.0-only Aurum POS software. The hosted Free tier permits 500 active inventory records in the primary shop. A valid Pro entitlement provides unlimited active inventory, up to three shops, and up to ten organization seats. Self-hosted deployments do not require a subscription.

Shop responsibilities

Shop owners are responsible for account access, the accuracy and lawful handling of customer and tax records, exports, and use of the service in their jurisdiction.
